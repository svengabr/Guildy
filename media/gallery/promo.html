Guildy

a guild window addon for WoW Forever
Make the guild window
worth opening again.
  • Chat first: the whole guild chat history with times, who's online, where and what they craft
  • Census: who's still active, classes, levels and professions at a glance
  • Professions: search a recipe, see who in the guild can make it
  • Hall of Fame: guild records and titles from character statistics
  • Character pages with twinks, dungeons and gear, notes and ranks right beside them
Lives in Blizzard's own guild window, J opens it as always.
Free · open source · CurseForge and GitHub
