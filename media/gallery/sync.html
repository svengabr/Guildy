How Guildy shares within your guild

No server, no account: Guildy users swap what they know over WoW’s hidden guild addon channel.
1

Everyone knows themselves

Open your profession windows once and Guildy knows your skills and recipes. It also reads your own statistics, gear and reputation. That’s all it needs.

2

Log in, compare notes

When you log in, Guildy tells the other Guildy users what it knows and how old it is. Whoever has something newer sends it over, in small portions, so nobody lags.

3

Nothing gets lost offline

Every Guildy user keeps what they’ve seen and passes it on. So Lunea’s recipes reach Bo even if they’re never online at the same time. Newest data wins, and every value shows its age.

Members without Guildy? Guildy still finds out:

  • Target them nearby: statistics and gear are read (about 28 yards)
  • Profession links they post in chat
  • Guild notes like “Herb/Alch”
  • “Read online members” opens their recipe books

Good to know

  • Only between members of your guild
  • A member’s own data beats passed-on data
  • Throttled: no lag, no disconnects
  • Sharing your own data can be switched off
